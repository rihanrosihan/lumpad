## Decisions this build forces (please read before approving Plan v2)

**The locked load guard, applied to the real numbers, cannot produce a plan that looks like the old one.** Starting from your actual 15.9 km/week (twin, 23 Nov) and the 2027 race calendar (Jakim B 13 Jun, MJM D 20 Jun, JRF A 24 Oct, Bormar C 14 Nov), the engine's weekly peak, with every guardrail on:

| Variant | Peak km/wk | Peak before Jakim | JRF build (Jul–Sep) | Checkpoints postponed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rule read literally ("+10 % vs the previous 4 calendar weeks"), cutback every 4th week ×0.9 (default) | 20.9 | 20.9 | 9.6 | 3 |
| Literal, no scheduled cutbacks | 25.8 | 25.8 | 12.8 | 2 |
| **Baseline = previous 4 build weeks** (planned down weeks excluded, DEC-9), cutback ×0.9 | 27.8 | 21.9 | 25.4 | 2 |
| Same, cutback ×0.75 | 27.8 | 21.9 | 25.4 | 2 |
| Same, no scheduled cutbacks | 34.9 | 25.8 | 33.2 | 1 |
| (old imported plan, for scale) | 96 | | | |

Why: "+10 % of the 4-week average" lets volume grow only about 4 %/week at best, and a taper or a recovery week drags the average down so the next build starts low. Three consecutive FM-distance races (Jakim, MJM a week later, then JRF and Bormar) each cost a taper and a recovery. A classic cutback every 4th week at ×0.75 produces **no growth at all** under the literal rule.

None of this is a generator quirk: it is arithmetic on the rules in `LOCKED_RULES`. The engine therefore ships the **literal** interpretation as the default (it must never weaken a locked rule, C-7) and offers the others as parameters that need your decision:

- **DEC-8:** race-day km exempt from the weekly guard (`raceExempt`). Without it, a marathon race week (42 km) breaks the guard in any realistic week. Used by the generator; off by default in the P0 kernel report so the 29-of-54 number is unchanged.
- **DEC-9:** guard baseline = previous 4 *build* weeks (`guard_baseline: 'build'` on the A goal's params). Planned down weeks (taper, recovery, race week, cutback) are not part of the baseline and are not judged.
- **DEC-10:** cutback policy (`cutback_ratio`, default 0.9 every 4th week; `peak_cap_km`, default 60).

Whether a 20–35 km/week plan can support the sub-4:00 goal is a coaching question for you (and the KB targets), not something the engine decides. The numbers above are the honest ceiling under the rules as they stand.

## What was built

`macro.js` (periodise → allocate → fill; `classify`, `weekTarget`, `planWeek`, `generate`, `checkCandidate`), `macroflow.js` (goals, availability, `proposeMacro`, `macroHold`, `handleMacroTriggers`), kernel options `raceExempt` and `guardBaseline`/`downWeeks`. Routes: GET `goals`, `availability`; PUT `goal`, `availability`; POST `goals/seed`, `macro` (writes need an authenticated user).

Design points: week volume = what the kernel will allow (not a target that is later repaired); actual placed km (not targets) feed the next week's guard; interval templates are sized in whole repetitions and record the km actually built; time trials and gates the load or the calendar cannot carry are postponed and reported (`conflicts`), never forced; the strictest applicable taper wins; long runs use the kernel's 30-day window; weeks are budgeted down to the days that exist (the plan ends on a Friday).

## Bugs found by the tests while building (all fixed)

Volume never grew under the 4-week average with cutbacks; quality sessions built more km than recorded; cross-week 48 h clashes; a C-race loosening the A-race taper; a time trial next to a race; long runs ignoring the 110 % window; rounding to 0.5 km silently costing ~1.5 km/week and compounding; kept-week history making the first week too generous.
